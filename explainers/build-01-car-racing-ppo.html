How to read it

Cyan is what the AI sees and does. Every frame, the game shows it the screen, the image network sums it up, and the actor picks steering, gas and brake. The critic guesses how many points are still coming.

Gold is the reward. The game hands out points after each action, and they go only into the memory of the drive. In the learning step, points earned minus the critic's guess gives the advantage: actions that did better than expected get more likely, worse ones less likely, and no single update can change the driver by more than about 20%.

That's PPO: the actor is the driver, the critic is the coach, and the rule is "improve, but not too fast."
